HUONG DAN THUYET TRINH DEMO - THANH VIEN 4
Chu de: Nguoi dung va tuong tac (Auth + Ho so Pet + Danh gia)

I. THONG TIN PHAN CONG
- Chuc nang 10: Tai khoan va phan quyen (feature/tv4-auth-security)
- Chuc nang 11: Ho so "Boss" (feature/tv4-pet-profile)
- Chuc nang 12: Danh gia va binh luan (feature/tv4-reviews-rating)

II. MUC TIEU DEMO
1. Chung minh he thong dang nhap/phan quyen chay dung:
- User vao duoc trang ca nhan.
- Admin vao duoc trang quan tri.

2. Chung minh User quan ly ho so thu cung, co the sua va UP ANH PET:
- Chon anh tu may tinh.
- Luu thanh cong vao database MySQL.
- Reload trang van giu duoc anh.

3. Chung minh quy trinh danh gia dung nghiep vu:
- Don hoan thanh moi duoc danh gia.
- User viet danh gia tuy y.
- Admin duyet danh gia.
- Sau duyet thi hien o "Danh gia cua toi" va trang chi tiet san pham.

III. KICH BAN DEMO CHI TIET
A. DEMO AUTH + PHAN QUYEN
B1. Mo 2 tab:
- Tab 1: Dang nhap user.
- Tab 2 (an danh): Dang nhap admin.

B2. Tai khoan demo:
- User: test1@gmail.com / (mat khau demo dang co)
- Admin: admin@petshop.com / admin123

B3. Trinh bay:
- User vao duoc cac trang profile, don hang, thu cung, danh gia.
- Admin vao duoc dashboard va cac trang quan tri (don hang, danh gia...).

B. DEMO HO SO BOSS + UP ANH PET
B1. O tab User -> vao "Thu cung cua toi".
B2. Bam icon Sua (cay but) tren 1 pet.
B3. Trong popup "Chinh sua thu cung":
- Nhan vao truong "Anh thu cung".
- Chon 1 anh tu may (JPG/PNG).
- Anh preview hien ngay trong form.

B4. Bam "Cap nhat".
B5. Reload trang (F5) va trinh bay:
- Anh pet van hien trong card thu cung.
- Nghia la du lieu anh da duoc luu vao MySQL (cot image cua bang pets).

B6. Neu can show DB:
- Mo MySQL Workbench.
- Chay: SELECT id, name, image FROM pets ORDER BY id DESC;
- Cho thay cot image da co gia tri.

C. DEMO DANH GIA & BINH LUAN END-TO-END
B1. O tab Admin:
- Vao Quan ly don hang.
- Day trang thai don theo luong: PENDING -> CONFIRMED -> PROCESSING -> SHIPPING -> DELIVERED.

B2. O tab User:
- Vao "Don hang cua toi".
- Don DELIVERED se co nut "Da nhan hang".
- Bam xac nhan -> don thanh COMPLETED.

B3. Sau khi COMPLETED:
- Trong tung san pham cua don se hien nut "Danh gia" (nut moi ben canh thao tac).
- Bam "Danh gia" -> mo form nhap sao + noi dung.
- Nhap binh luan tuy y, vi du: "San pham dung mo ta, giao nhanh, se ung ho tiep".
- Bam gui.

B4. O tab Admin -> Quan ly danh gia:
- Thay danh gia vua gui o trang thai cho duyet.
- Bam "Duyet" de cho hien cong khai.
- Co the Reply neu muon.

B5. Quay lai tab User:
- Vao "Danh gia cua toi" -> thay danh gia vua duoc duyet.
- Mo trang chi tiet san pham -> thay danh gia xuat hien o tab Danh gia.
